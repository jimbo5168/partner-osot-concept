      “Partner numbers don't match across reports.”
      Heard from partner sales, operations and finance analysts in the same week. Three different meanings.
      vagueurgentmulti-team
      
      
      User problem
      A partner manager cannot tell which revenue number is the one to quote.
      Same partner appears under four names across CRM, billing and incentives, so roll-ups disagree.
      whowhencost of the gap
      
      
      Requirements
      One golden partner ID, one revenue definition, one freshness promise.
      • Data contract for partner revenue v1
      • Match precision of at least 98% on audited sample
      • Freshness under 6 hours, flagged when late
      
      
      Measurable outcome
      Report disagreement from 0 to under 0.
      Plus time to answer a partner revenue question, from 2 days to same day. Synthetic targets.
