Partner OSOTa product owner's 90-day plan

Six authoritative systems, one governed partner truth. This walkthrough shows, step by step, how I would own the strategy, the backlog, the technical trade-offs and the production health of a partner single source of truth, and how I would know it is working.

The plan at a glance

Listen, decide, then prove it with numbers.

  1. Days 1–30

    Listen and baseline

    • 18 discovery interviews across the 6 user groups
    • Baseline match rate, freshness and report-dispute volume
    • Map the source of record for every core partner attribute
    • Inventory conflicting metric definitions

  2. Days 31–60

    Decide and commit

    • Publish strategy, outcomes and a Now / Next / Later roadmap
    • Sign the first data contract with source-system owners
    • Decision records for identity, freshness and access
    • Prioritized, public backlog with acceptance criteria

  3. Days 61–90

    Ship and prove

    • Golden partner ID v1 for the top 2,000 partners
    • Freshness SLO alerts live on the top 5 sources
    • Certified definitions discoverable in the catalog
    • First outcome review with leadership: met, or why not

Prioritization, made explicit

Every candidate is scored on partner-business value, trust (quality and governance), platform risk reduced and the work it unblocks, minus effort. Switch the lens to see how the order changes, and click any item for the trade-off I would explain to stakeholders.

    Problem statement

    Partner teams cannot trust attained-revenue numbers in partner reviews, because the same partner appears under different IDs across source systems and each report applies its own definition.

    User scenario · quarterly partner review prep

    Alex, partner development manager, top-20 systems integrator

    Target

    1. Opens the certified Partner Account 360 view
    2. Sees freshness and the definition on hover
    3. Shares one number with the partner team
    4. Prep in under 2 hours
