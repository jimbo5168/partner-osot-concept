Every product, every agent.

A platform-and-data concept built around one job description: a shared metric layer, a trust layer for third-party location data, and permission-aware context for AI agents, so every product gives a customer the same defensible answer.

Requirement 1 Product ownership of a shared platform

A platform roadmap that product teams can plan against.

JD: "Experience: 7+ years in product management, including significant ownership of enterprise platform, data, or other complex B2B products."

Platform vision

Every product and every agent reads the same governed metric, knows how far to trust it, and only sees what the customer is entitled to. New analytics ship in days, not quarters.

  • Usability same number in every view
  • Growth new products start on shared metrics
  • Delivery one pipeline instead of seven
  • Value fewer "numbers don't match" escalations

      Requirement 5 Delivery and product judgment

      Move existing workflows without breaking a customer's numbers.

      JD: "Proven track record of delivering complex platform initiatives across teams, managing dependencies and risks, and making sound customer, business, scope, timing, and resource trade-offs."

      Wave 0weeks 1 to 6

      Foundations

      Metric layer, trust scoring and a parity harness that replays legacy queries.

      • Exit aadt.v3 within ±2% of legacy on 500 benchmark segments
      • Depends on partner schema contracts
      Wave 1weeks 5 to 12

      Traffic Counts

      Shadow mode, four weeks of dual-run, then cutover with a "what changed" note in the product.

      • Exit zero unexplained differences above 2%
      • Risk customers see numbers shift; release notes and a side-by-side view
      Wave 2weeks 10 to 18

      Safety

      Crash-rate denominators switch to aadt.v3; entitlements move into the metric API.

      • Depends on entitlements service
      • Risk crash-data join keys differ; mapping table owned by platform
      Wave 3weeks 16 to 24

      Construction Impact

      Custom zones use the on-demand path from ADR-007; event calendar becomes a shared input.

      • Depends on on-demand compute budget
      • Risk cost spikes on large zones; per-query cap and cache
      Wave 4weeks 22 to 30

      Agent GA, retire legacy

      Agent passes the evaluation gate; six legacy pipelines are switched off with owners' sign-off.

      • Exit eval gate green two releases in a row
      • Risk a team keeps its copy; retirement date agreed up front

      Dependencies and risks

      • High
        Partner contract renewal mid-plan

        Orbit's terms renew in week 14. Mitigation: trust layer already scores a fallback blend without it.

      • High
        Visible number shifts

        Customers notice. Mitigation: dual-run, "what changed" panel, customer success briefed one wave ahead.

      • Med
        Shared engineering capacity

        Safety's own roadmap competes for the same team. Mitigation: Wave 2 scope agreed with its PM at planning.

      • Med
        Agent quality regressions

        Model or prompt changes. Mitigation: golden set runs on every release; gate blocks.
